Explanations: "Hit dice" specifies the number of
eight-sided dice rolled to determine how many
hits you can take before dying. The sum of the
rolls is modified by additions/subtractions due to
your constitution.

Spells -- Magical and Clerical

As your experience increases you will be able to
use more spells in each trip. E.g., if your quota
(magical) is given as '3+1', you can use 3 first-
level spells and 1 second-level spell during the
dungeon trip. (Note that you are assumed to ‘know’
all the spells; the limit is on your memory and
energy available.)

Magic:    Level 1                Level 2

    1.    Sleep                  ESP
    2.    Charm                  Speed
    3.    Light                  Invisibility
    4.    Magic Missile          Blastbolt

Clerical: Level 1                Level 2

    1.    Cure Wounds            Serious Cure
    2.    Detect Evil            Hold Person
    3.    Protection from Evil   Continual Light
    4.    Pray                   Dispel Myth

The magic spells do the following:

Sleep -- Puts an opponent to sleep. There is no
chance of failure, but it works only on enemies up
to level 4, and not at all on undead.

Charm -- Opponent is hypnotized into killing him-
self. Spell may fail. Works on any level, but only
on men and goblin-types.

Light* -- Casts light around you, reduces your
chance of being surprised by 1/3. (24 turns)

Magic Missile -- Creates a magic arrow that tries
to strike your enemy. Damage 2-7 hits if it strikes
(double damage on undead) .

ESP* -- eliminates surprise by any but undead, since
the latter have no ‘brain-waves'.

Speed -- Doubles your movement/action rate for 24
turns; guarantees first strike at any opponent.

Invisibility* -- Useful for flight from disaster.
Lasts 4 turns. You may not attack while invisible.

Blastbolt -- A last-gasp shot at a superior enemy,
it puts 5-30 hits on him (3-15 if he is able to
duck part of it). This spell exhausts all your
remaining energy for magic in the given trip.

The clerical spells are as follows:

Cure Wounds: Replaces 2-7 hits that you have lost
in battle. Of course, you can't get more than you
started with.
(Serious Cure is the same, but 4-14 hits.)

Detect Evil*: Eliminates chance of surprise except
by animals (which are neutral). Runs 36 turns.

Protection from Evil: Reduces the chance of being
struck by an enemy by 5% (10% undead). 24 turns.

Pray: Increases your chance of striking an enemy
by 5% (10% undead). Lasts one combat.

Hold: This clerical ‘charm’ is 10% more likely to
succeed than the magical Charm spell. Only men and
goblin-types can be held.

Continual Light* -- Reduces your chance of being
surprised by 2/3. Lasts the entire trip.

Dispel Myth: Disturbs mythical monsters (only) when
you refuse to believe in them. They may leave in
disgust! Not too effective on dragons, who are very
sure of themselves.
